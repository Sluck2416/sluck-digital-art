About the artist

Faces, light & noise.

I’m Sluck, an independent content creator and self-taught digital artist working out of Bengaluru.

I started drawing digitally about two years ago and never stopped. Most of my work is portraits and character art, shaped by K-drama faces, anime line work and the colour of late-night city streets. I also edit, build small games in Roblox and run a commission shop.

Philosophy. Have fun and keep smiling.
